SkyShiftSPHEREx time-lapse explorer

Watch the infrared sky move.

Since 2025, NASA's SPHEREx telescope has mapped the whole sky every six months in 102 colours of infrared light. SkyShift turns those images into a time-lapse of any spot you choose. Stars that sit still stay white; anything that moves, flickers, or glows strangely in the infrared stands out — just like Pluto did in 1930.

  1. Pick a spot — type a name or coordinates.
  2. Watch it blink — epochs six months apart play as a movie.
  3. Let SkyShift measure — motion, brightness changes and a full 0.75–5 µm spectrum, computed live in your browser.

Pixels stream straight from NASA's public SPHEREx archive on AWS — no account, no install. Works with every new data release automatically.

—

Tip: click any star to get its spectrum, brightness history and motion. Arrow keys step, space plays.
